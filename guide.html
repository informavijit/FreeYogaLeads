1

Setup & Business Profile Configuration

Takes 2 minutes. Configures your identity so all AI outreach messages sound authentic.

How to Do It:

  1. Click on "Settings & AI Key" in the top-right corner.
  2. Fill in your Brand Name (e.g. Bodhi School of Yoga).
  3. Fill in your Founder Name and WhatsApp Number (where inquiries should arrive).
  4. Paste your Google Gemini Free API Key (optional). You can get one free from Google AI Studio.
  5. Click "Save Settings".
Crucial Security Note on API Key:

Never hardcode your Gemini API Key in the source code! Even if your GitHub repository is private, GitHub Pages serves code directly to client browsers where anyone can view it via Developer Tools. The app safely stores your key in your browser's private localStorage only.

No API key? No problem! The built-in smart templates generate battle-tested pitches with zero setup.

Directory Finder (Google Maps B2B Mining)

Target Gyms, Fitness Studios, Coworking Spaces & Wellness Centers.

This tab uses the logic of gosom/Google-Maps-Scraper to harvest businesses whose trainers can be upsold to Yoga Teacher Training Courses (YTTC).

1. Choose Category

Gyms & Fitness Centres: Prime target! Gym trainers want Yoga Alliance certifications to charge 2x per hour.
Coworking Hubs: Target for Corporate Desk Yoga workshops.

2. Enter City & Country

Type any city (e.g. Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Dubai, London) or click one of the quick preset buttons.

3. Click "Find Free Leads"

The app queries the live database and populates a table with Business Names, Addresses, Verified Phones, and Websites.

What to Do With Each Lead in the Table:

  • Sparkles Icon (): Sends this gym straight to the AI Outreach Studio with their phone number pre-filled.
  • Plus Icon (): Saves this business into your CRM pipeline for follow-up.
  • "Save All to CRM": Saves all 20+ leads into your CRM in one click.

Social Intent Hunter & Custom Free-Text Search

Capture individuals actively searching for Yoga Teacher Training or Online Yoga Coaching.

This tab uses the signal detection philosophy of codex-first-customer-finder-skill. Instead of advertising to cold audiences, it listens to people who already raised their hand publicly.

1. Running a Search (Two Ways):

  • Option A - Dropdown Presets: Choose quick topics like Yoga Teacher Training or Online Yoga for Back Pain.
  • Option B - Custom Free-Text Search: Type any custom keyword or phrase into the text box (e.g. 200 hour online certification USA, private yoga instructor back pain). It overrides the dropdown automatically without requiring any code edits!

2. Understanding Contact Details on Each Card:

  • ✉️ Email Badge: If the user shared an email in their post, click to launch your email client immediately.
  • 📞 Phone / WhatsApp: Opens a direct WhatsApp chat.
  • 💬 1-Click Reddit DM: Directly opens Reddit's message composer targeting that exact username!
The Golden Rule for Social Outreach: Never paste a spammy buy-now link! Click "Draft AI Response" to generate an authentic response that answers their question, mentions your Rishikesh lineage/Yoga Alliance accreditation, and offers a free syllabus or posture check.

IVR Voice Studio (India Lead Automation)

Multi-Lingual 7 Languages

Qualify Indian leads automatically with native interactive voice calling before spending ad money.

In India, cold outbound calling and IVR has 5x to 8x higher conversion than cold emails. The IVR Studio lets you design your call script, questions, and automated responses online, supporting: Hindi (हिंदी), English, Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Malayalam (മലയാളം), and Bengali (বাংলা).

1. Multi-Lingual Designer

Click any language tab (Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, etc.). Customize the welcome script and responses for Keys 1, 2, 3, 9, and 0. Click "Save Custom Script".

2. Place Real Outbound Call

Click "📞 Place Outbound IVR Call" or "📱 Direct Dial (SIM)". Features smart call termination: if no input for 10 seconds, the call politely disconnects (Cold Lead). When an option is chosen, the IVR announces "One of our Coordinators will call you back" in their chosen language and cleanly disconnects!

3. Test Audio (TTS)

Click "🎧 Test Audio (Browser TTS)" to listen to the speech synthesis and voice accents locally in your web browser before dialing real customers.

4. 1-Click Dial from CRM

In Directory Finder and CRM Pipeline, every Indian lead (+91) has a "📞 IVR Call" badge. Clicking it immediately opens the dialer ready to connect!

Automatic Lead Temperature Classification (Hot, Warm, Cold) Real-Time CRM Tagging
🔥 Hot Leads (Priority 1)

• Pressed Key 1 (Yoga Teacher Training - YTTC 200/300hr)
• Pressed Key 0 (Live Call Transfer to Counselor)
• Auto-marked as Trial Booked with ₹45,000 deal value!

☀️ Warm Leads (Priority 2)

• Pressed Key 2 (Personal Yoga Therapy / Classes)
• Pressed Key 9 (WhatsApp Syllabus & Fee Brochure)
• Pressed Key 3 (Corporate Wellness Workshop)

❄️ Cold Leads (Priority 3)

• Answered call but hung up without pressing a key
• Pressed an unmapped or invalid digit
• Queued for automatic SMS/WhatsApp re-engagement

How Bulk Outbound IVR Calling Works (Step-by-Step):

  1. Scroll down to the "Bulk IVR Outbound Campaign Launcher" section.
  2. Paste your 50 Indian phone numbers into the box, or click "Load 50 Sample Indian Leads".
  3. Check the "Live Telephony Dispatcher" banner above the list. It automatically connects to the local carrier bridge on http://localhost:5000 or your saved Exotel/Webhook gateway.
  4. Enable "🔊 Live Audio Monitor" if you want to hear real dialing ringtones and DTMF tone responses as each call connects!
  5. Choose your pace: "Realistic Live Call" (~2.5s per lead with live cadence) or "Fast Cloud Batch".
  6. Click "📞 Launch Outbound Bulk IVR Calls (50 Numbers)".
  7. Every call goes through: Outbound Ringing ➔ Handset Answered ➔ Multi-Lingual Prompt Played ➔ Return Response DTMF Captured. All results are saved directly to your Lead CRM with Hot / Warm / Cold badges and direct "📞 Dial Live" shortcuts!

Cloud Telephony Gateway Configuration (Exotel, Twilio & Webhooks):

Click "Gateway Settings" beside the phone input or in the Telephony card below. You can enter your Exotel Account SID, API Token, and Virtual Caller DID (+91 80 4719 8800), or supply a Webhook URL (n8n, Make, Zapier, MyOperator, Sarv).

💡 Zero-Cost Sandbox Mode: If you do not have an Exotel account yet, you can still test calling immediately! The built-in Instant Cloud Dispatch Sandbox provides realistic telecom handshakes (ringing, answered, DTMF tone playback, and automatic CRM classification) completely free with ₹0 spend!

Lead CRM Pipeline & CSV Export

Track prospects through your sales funnel to enrollment.

Every lead from Directory, Social Intent, or IVR calls is saved directly to your local CRM. Leads go through 5 stages and 3 temperature tiers:

1. New
2. Contacted
3. Trial Booked
4. Enrolled / Won
5. Inactive

Filtering by Lead Temperature:

Use the top filter buttons in the CRM table to slice your database:

  • 🔥 Hot Leads: Prospects ready for high-ticket certification or live enrollment calls. Prioritize your morning outreach here.
  • ☀️ Warm Leads: Inquiries for personal therapy classes or prospects who requested WhatsApp syllabus. Send trial class reminders.
  • ❄️ Cold Leads: Dropped calls or unanswered leads. Automate follow-up messages or re-queue.

Exporting to Google Sheets or Excel

Click "Export CSV" to download a clean spreadsheet containing all contact numbers, temperatures, notes, and statuses.
